# figrip evaluation on the real library, 2026-10

Evaluator report. Nothing in `figrip/` was changed; the numbers below are from the code as of 2026-10-08.
Re-run with `python -I tools/eval_library.py --sheets` (see the end of this file).

## 1. Method

* Library: `D:/Zotero/storage/*/*.pdf` (376 files). Scan = pages where `sum(len(d["items"]))` over `get_drawings()` is > 800:
  172 files have at least one such page (the "173" in ARCHITECTURE.md).
* Sample: 60 pages from 40 files (one page per file first, then second pages; duplicates by file size skipped; pages that
  mention Figure / J-V / EQE / Voltage / Wavelength preferred). Two of the 40 files are copies of the same paper
  (Fan 2024, different sizes), so there are 39 distinct papers.
* Every page was run through `figrip.pipeline.extract(pdf, pages=[i])` in its own process with a 60 s timeout.
* **Every full page (70 dpi) and all 164 panel overlays were looked at by eye** (contact sheets: full page + overlays).
  Each found panel got one grade:
  * **OK** - tick values right, every real curve is a series whose points sit on it, no merged series, no polluted series list.
    Up to a few tiny (<= 12 pt) legend-swatch series are tolerated and counted separately.
  * **calib-wrong** - tick values / scale wrong.
  * **series-wrong** - calibration right, but a real curve is missing or merged or mapped through the wrong axis, or the series list is
    polluted (>= 6 spurious series, or >= 2 spurious series with > 12 pts).
  * **false-panel** - not a (vector) x-y plot, or no data can come out of it.
* "True plots" = x-y plots whose data are vector paths. Plots whose data are a placed raster image (22 plots on 6 pages) are
  excluded from the denominator and reported separately (section 4).
  Stacked sub-plots count one by one; colour maps / CIE diagrams / energy-level diagrams / bar-of-energies are not counted.

Reading it: the pipeline never falls over, finds almost every plot whose axes have numeric tick labels, and the axis maths is very good.
The weak part is **series extraction**: 35 % of the found panels have a wrong series list. Hard numbers (tick pairs, ranges) are trustworthy
in nearly every panel, the curves are not.

Types seen **not** at all in this sample: bar charts, box plots, GIWAXS line cuts (only one XRD with log-y and one R-SoXS profile, the latter raster).
Treat those as untested, not as working.

Not a figrip failure but worth knowing: of the 60 "vector-heavy" pages, 10 contain no vector plot (5 title/text pages with logos or
outlined glyphs, 5 pages whose figures are placed bitmaps; plus one graphical-abstract page). The `items > 800` filter therefore over-counts vector-figure pages by about 17 %;
outlined (Type3-like) body text inflates it. Zero false panels came from chemical structures, device sketches, energy diagrams or tables.

## 3. Failure taxonomy (ordered by impact)

"Panels" = number of found panels hit (a panel is counted once, under its main cause). Modules are my best guess from the symptoms and
a couple of probes (paths / box candidates dumped with the figrip internals; nothing modified).
PDF pointers are `D:/Zotero/storage/<KEY>/*.pdf`, 1-based page, panel bbox in page points as printed in the overlay.

### Series-level failures (57 panels = 35 % of found)

**S1. Fragmented or duplicated curves (18 panels).** One curve comes out as several series (pieces of a dashed/dotted line, line and its markers
extracted separately, each curve twice), so a panel with 4 curves lists 9-19 series. Data points are on the curves, the series list is unusable
without hand clean-up. Module: `series.py` (grouping of paths of the same colour/style into one series).
Repro: `Y83BF4NF` p19 panel#3 bbox [405.8, 66.7, 551.8, 185.8] (18 series, 4 curves); `Y83BF4NF` p20 panel#5 [229.0, 208.7, 375.1, 327.2];
`YKMSESKV` p26 panel#1 [129.2, 74.3, 292.9, 181.5] (7 series, 2 curves).

**S2. Real curve or markers dropped (15 panels).** Four distinct causes, all end in missing data:
(a) a line drawn as many separate 1-segment strokes (grey line in `YSCDXAHA`, upper line in `CSYW58JA` Fig. 2f) is never chained and vanishes;
(b) a jagged curve that is a *filled outline* (226-segment polygon) collapses to a 2-point series;
(c) open markers (circles/squares/triangles drawn as stroked outlines) and error-bar plots give nothing, or only a few points;
(d) sparse marker curves (a few big markers on a thin line) give 5-20 points and tick marks as extra series.
Module: `series.py` (outline-to-centreline, chaining of 1-segment strokes, marker detection).
Repro: `YSCDXAHA` p14 panel#1 [154.4, 65.2, 493.4, 149.9] (grey reference line missing, only the orange one);
`ZJRX9AAG` p2 panel#1 [344.0, 233.1, 529.6, 354.9] (red AM1.5 spectrum -> 2 pts);
`KAR4TPN7` p8 panel#3 [118.3, 225.0, 221.4, 310.1] (open-circle data of the Mott-Schottky plot not extracted, only the fit lines).
More: `HZW35VXU` p7 panels#1,#2 (open markers), `KXIKDYIH` p5 panels#1,#2,#4,#5 (sparse markers + ticks as series), `CSYW58JA` p5 panel#3 (error bars, 2 pts).

**S3. Twin y axis: right-axis curves mapped through the left axis (10 panels).** The right axis is detected in a few cases and only
logged as "twin y axis ... ignored", but its curves are still emitted with *left-axis values* - silently wrong numbers, e.g. integrated Jsc
shown as EQE %. In 5 of the 10 panels there is not even a note. Module: `axes.py` (detect/own the right axis, attach a second `AxisCalib`),
then `series.py` (assign series to the axis they belong to, or drop them).
Repro: `B84X8NKW` p14 panel#2 [318.6, 184.9, 488.6, 321.9] (EQE + integrated Jsc); `H8NEBZ3S` p4 panel#2 [390.3, 75.3, 518.8, 179.7];
`HZW35VXU` p7 panel#3 [148.1, 195.1, 275.3, 303.3] (Jsc / PCE vs time, no note at all).

**S4. One marker = one series (6 panels).** Open markers drawn as small stroked polylines (21-vertex circles, 4-13 vertex squares and
triangles) are not recognised as markers, so every marker becomes its own series (23-77 series per panel). Module: `series.py` (marker
recognition for stroked/outlined symbols; the filled-circle case already works, see `marker_pts` in other panels).
Repro: `ZSAKRA3X` p3 panel#1 [104.3, 69.5, 225.5, 168.6] (32 series of 21 pts); `7TGFBS3U` p3 panel#1 [358.5, 220.9, 521.6, 364.8] (77 series).

**S5. Spurious series from non-data graphics (6 panels).** Text glyphs outlined into paths (peak labels "(110)", "(311)" -> 65 series),
a molecule drawing or equivalent-circuit inset inside the plot frame (20+ series), dashed annotation ellipses, filled-region outlines.
(Panels with only 2-4 tiny legend-swatch series are graded OK but see C2.) Module: `series.py` (reject paths that are glyphs, closed
tiny shapes, or in an inset that is not data; legend/inset masking).
Repro: `PBYLXZBV` p3 panel#3 [343.7, 574.7, 495.1, 687.5] (XRD: 1 real series + 65 glyph series);
`J4FI5SLT` p3 panel#2 [243.9, 175.2, 359.1, 267.8] (PC71BM structure drawn in the J-V frame);
`YSCDXAHA` p3 panel#4 [121.5, 249.4, 223.2, 331.3] (equivalent circuit inset -> 12 two-point series).

**S6. Same-colour curves merged (1 panel) and one spurious straight series (1 panel).** Black curves told apart only by marker shape end in one
series with both curves (`CSYW58JA` p3 panel#2 [242.5, 80.9, 368.1, 173.1]); a 71-marker diagonal across a transmittance plot
(`7TGFBS3U` p4 panel#1 [342.8, 278.7, 509.8, 427.1]). Module: `series.py`.

### Panel-level failures

**P1. Frame found but the y axis is not calibrated -> plot missed (8 plots; the biggest recall loss that is fixable).**
The frame box exists, text labels exist, but the left axis ends with no calibration, so the panel is dropped. Probes show the left-side
tick search returns 2 "major" ticks (one spurious) while 5 aligned tick labels are present (negative J-V labels "-15 -10 -5 0 5" in two
cases); in another the labels are outlined glyphs with minus signs; in another each label is "1.5 x 10^28" with the exponent attached.
Module: `axes.py` (`_find_ticks` / `_pair_labels` / `parse_value`).
Repro: `NPBLA25U` p3 Fig. 2A bbox (145, 560, 292, 673); `TTLJSBH4` p7 Fig. S-10a (112, 90, 263, 202);
`KAR4TPN7` p8 Fig. 3B (271, 109, 363, 194) and 3F (401, 225, 504, 310) (the same figure sits in a second copy, 2 more misses);
`E68U3HSK` p7 Fig. 5b (329, 70, 487, 209); `ZSAKRA3X` p3 Fig. 3a (146, 540, 305, 680).

**P2. Plots with no numeric y tick labels (8 plots: XRD, UPS, XPS in arbitrary units).** Never found; by design nothing to calibrate.
Worth a decision: x-only calibration with y in "a.u." (relative) would recover them. Module: `axes.py` (policy) + `model.py` (optional y calib).
Repro: `PBYLXZBV` p4 (53, 59, 266, 231) and (53, 264, 278, 434); `9K8P64N7` p3 (377, 56, 513, 155); `45DNDAWI` p3 XRD (307, 55, 488, 209).

**P3. Categorical x axis (2 plots).** "Ref., KCl-5, KCl-8..." or "1:1.3, 1:2, 1:3" instead of numbers. Module: `axes.py`.
Repro: `9K8P64N7` p3 (379, 338, 517, 441); `WKTIJPRE` p4 panel d (341, 234, 476, 344).

**P4. Stacked small plots and plots without a frame (5 plots).** Four stability plots stacked in one column share one tall box
(`A2JEDV2B` p2 (438, 484, 535, 657)); a normalized-absorbance plot with a pink UV/IR band across its frame produced no frame box at all
(`E68U3HSK` p4 Fig. 1a). Module: `axes.py` (`_split_at_dividers`, `_corner_boxes`).

**P5. False panels (9).** 4 colour maps with calibrated axes (`277VSDFR` p8 panel#5 [247.3, 245.6, 353.4, 348.3]; `J4FI5SLT` p4 panels#1,#2),
3 plots whose curves are a placed bitmap while the axes are vector (`5EX3UD7H` p2 panel#3 histogram [235.8, 200.8, 362.6, 294.2];
`WKTIJPRE` p2 panel#1 [187.6, 578.2, 284.8, 656.9]; `YSCDXAHA` p3 panel#1 [121.5, 131.1, 223.0, 212.7] - the pipeline even notes
"plot area is covered by a raster image" and still emits a series list), 2 graphical-abstract thumbnails (`E5C63GH3` p1 panels#1,#2, 45 and 18
junk series). Module: `axes.py`/`pipeline.py` (suppress panels with the raster note or tiny size; drop colour maps).

**P6. Axis calibration wrong (2 panels).** A histogram whose tick labels are partly read gives x 14.3..15.3 and 15.8..18.3 for a true
14.5..18.0 axis, and the same histogram is returned twice (`A2JEDV2B` p2 panel#3 [306.9, 580.9, 334.2, 655.8], panel#4 [336.0, 580.9, 407.6, 655.8]).
Module: `axes.py` (OCR of crowded labels; fit should reject when residual or label coverage is poor) - this is the only wrong calibration in 154 real plots.

### Cosmetic / minor (not counted as failures)

* **C1. Legend names are matched for only part of the series**: 92 of 164 panels have at least one real curve (> 20 pts) still called `seriesN`;
  some names look shifted (Fujii Fig. S4 panels). Module: `series.py` legend matching.
* **C2. Legend swatches and tick marks as tiny series in otherwise OK panels**: 26 of the 96 OK panels (2-12 pt series).
* **C3. Axis titles are missing for 51 panels** (x) / 49 (y): every glyph/OCR-calibrated page has no `label`; panel letter wrong once
  (`J4FI5SLT` p3, d reported as c). Module: `axes.py` `_titles`.

## 4. What works, what does not

* **Works** (OK in the sample): linear and log J-V (light and dark, Jph-Veff), EQE/IPCE/IQE lines with colour-distinguished curves, absorption /
  transmittance / absorption-coefficient spectra (curves with 8 000+ points are fine), AM1.5 vs lamp spectra, Nyquist plots, single-curve TPC/PL
  decays, Voc-vs-intensity and Jsc-vs-intensity log plots, 3x3 grids of panels (all 9 found, axes right), axis labels in outlined glyphs
  (46 panels calibrated from glyph shapes or pixel OCR, all right except the crowded histogram), reversed axes, offset multipliers ("x 10^4").
* **Does not**: twin-axis plots (most EQE + integrated-Jsc figures), open-marker data sets, error-bar plots, curves told apart by marker shape in black,
  stability curves with markers, histograms, colour maps (should be rejected), any plot whose y axis has no numbers.
* **Raster plots**: 22 plots (2 of them CIE diagrams) on 12 pages (whole figures in `EGAI2D2Q` p10, `KAYKZITT` p24/p25, `SC86JQZF` p4, `ZB9X2TNE` p4; single panels in
  `5EX3UD7H` p2, `WKTIJPRE` p2, `YSCDXAHA` p3, `CSYW58JA` p5, `DA23ILDA` p3, `E68U3HSK` p7, `2AHBRGZM` p6) are placed images; figrip cannot recover them and the pipeline is right to find nothing. Wanted: the CLI/scan should say "raster figure"
  instead of silence. Three of them show up as false panels (P5).

## 6. Priorities for the fixers (impact / effort guess)

1. `axes.py` P1 (8 plots, the frame is already there), then P6 (crowded-label calibration), then raster/colour-map suppression (P5).
2. `series.py` S2 + S1 + S4 (marker recognition for stroked symbols; chaining 1-segment strokes; grouping), they cover 39 of the 57 series-wrong panels.
3. Twin axis (S3) across `axes.py` + `series.py`: at least stop emitting wrongly-valued series.
4. Decide policy for no-y-tick plots (P2) and categorical x (P3).

## 7. Re-running the evaluation

```
python -I tools/eval_library.py --library "<your glob>/*.pdf" --max-pages 60 --out out/eval --sheets
```

`results.json` (per page: timing, panels, axis ranges and tick pairs, calibration source, series with counts, tracebacks), `png/` (full page
at 70 dpi + one overlay per panel), `sheets/` (one contact sheet per page, the thing to look at), `index.md` (one row per panel). The
selection is stored in `selection.json` so a rerun after a fix evaluates exactly the same pages; use `--reselect` for a new sample. The
grading itself is manual: open the sheets, then compare against the tables above (grades used here: OK / series-wrong / calib-wrong / false-panel).
